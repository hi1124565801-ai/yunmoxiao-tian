I'm easily distracted by a good story, an unfamiliar street, or a conversation that takes an unexpected turn. I like asking questions, sometimes more than people expect me to.

I'm interested in the things people care about, the peculiarities of everyday life, and all the little worlds that exist alongside my own.

I read, write, take photographs, and occasionally turn my curiosities into research projects.
